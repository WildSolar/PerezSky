Generated by `validation/validate.py`.

## 1. Parameters and luminous efficacy vs. Radiance gendaylit

86 sky conditions compared (solar altitude 5-80 deg, overcast to clear, two azimuths), with gendaylit's settings (Kasten 1966 air mass, E0 = 1367 W/m2, day number 0 with `-ang`, no cap on b).

- Max. absolute difference of the parameters a..e: 2.5e-06 (gendaylit prints 6 decimals)
- Max. relative difference of the diffuse luminous efficacy: 5.8e-04 (gendaylit prints the normalisation with 4 significant digits)
- Max. absolute difference of the direct luminous efficacy: 0.04 lm/W

gendaylit refused 2 conditions ("positive Perez parameter B, printing error sky", i.e. a black sky). perezsky computes b > 0 for exactly these and caps it at 0 by default:

- altitude 5 deg, DNI 850, DHI 60 W/m2: b = +0.0035
- altitude 5 deg, DNI 900, DHI 30 W/m2: b = +0.0176
